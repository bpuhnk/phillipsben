## What it is.

A full conversion of a stock Anet A8 into a BLV AM8: aluminum extrusions, linear
rails on all axes, a proper bed, and Marlin 2 with input shaping.

## What broke.

The original Anet A8 printed every bracket needed to upgrade itself, which is
the kind of joke that only lands once you watch a 36-hour print finish on the
machine you're about to retire.

## Where it lives now.

The garage. It prints the daily-driver work — enclosures, brackets, smart-home
mounts. The Ender 3 sits next to it; the K1 is fast and unrepentant.
